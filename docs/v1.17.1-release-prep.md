## 2. Production diagnostic-switch audit

`grep` of every `std::env::var` across production paths
(`src/main.rs`, `src/mnf8.rs`, `src/mix_coder.rs`, `src/mnf9.rs`,
`src/lib.rs`), excluding `cfg(test)` scopes:

| env var | where | effect | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `MNF17_P1D6_STATS` | `src/main.rs:309` (OnceLock, read once) | prints a `[d6]` counters line to stdout | print-only; no output/wire effect |
| `MNF17_P1D6_EXHAUSTIVE` | `src/mnf8.rs:682` (OnceLock, read once) | run the exhaustive instrument inside the same binary | byte-neutral: dominated arms can never win; asserted by `d6_pick_parity` |
| `MNF15_P1D_BODY_CAPS` | inside `#[cfg(test)]` block only | — | cfg(test)-only by construction |
| `P1B_UPDATE`, `MNF17_*` cohort drivers, `MNF17_P1D6_PHASE_*` | `cfg(test)` mods | research instruments | test-only |

Both production-visible switches are read once per process (the
per-mix-arm read that existed during D6-A was hoisted to a
`std::sync::OnceLock` before the bump). Dispositions, per the
publication clarification:

- `MNF17_P1D6_EXHAUSTIVE` is a **search-execution override**: it makes
  the selector evaluate every candidate to completion (no early
  certificates) inside the same binary. Its byte-neutrality is verified
  structurally (a dominated candidate can never win) and empirically
  (`d6_pick_parity`: production vs override pick — identical sizes,
  digests and backends). It is not print-only.
- `MNF17_P1D6_STATS` is print-only (stdout counters line).

Neither is consulted while building bytes, and normal execution (env
unset) performs no instrumentation. No undocumented production
configuration exists.

## 3. D6 record correction — mix-rate reconciliation (as ordered)

The D6 record's Hubble line "mix work ≈ 19 s of the wall" is RETRACTED:
it was derived by dividing the counters' 317.0M executed mix symbols by
a mis-converted C6 throughput figure. The correct, measured basis:

- **Marginal cost of one mix symbol (release, min of 3 reps,
  production-exact paths): 0.785–0.789 µs/symbol** on both stream
  scales (gradient 231,908 symbols: 191.7 ms full arm; Hubble
  2,621,402 symbols: 2,068.0 ms; two-point slope 0.785 µs; per-arm
  fixed ≈ 9.6 ms). Corroborated by the C6 192-arm cohort (49.91 s
  ≈ 0.83 µs/symbol) and the D1 census wall (192.2 s / 239.26 M ≈
  0.80 µs including builds+fixed).
- **Budget (terminating) form vs exhaustive form: equal within noise**
  (completing with the stride-256 check live: 194 vs 196 ms gradient,
  2,103 vs 2,099 ms Hubble) — the instrument adds no measurable
  per-symbol cost, so the D1 census's 0.0004% probe overhead claim
  carries over to the shipped loop.
- **Symbol accounting stands as measured**: corpus 199,746,476 of
  480,825,632 candidate mix symbols certified away (41.5%); Hubble
  317,001,180 executed vs 335,627,248 would-be (5.5% avoided).
- **Hubble decomposition (measured pieces, not projection)**:
  builds 8 × (3.9 s zstd-proxy + 9.0 s zlib-proxy) ≈ 100 s; cheap
  zstd19+zlib arms ≈ 6.5 s; mix work 317.0 M × ~0.79 µs ≈ 250 s;
  remainder ≈ assembly/relabels/interleave. The definitive
  terminated-vs-exhaustive Hubble comparison is the matched median
  series: **arm B 380.24 vs arm C 371.75 user s (medians of 3,
  alternating order — `docs/benchmarks-v1.17.1/d6-hubble-medians.log`;
  certified termination = −8.5 s = −2.3% of Hubble)**, not a
  composition. Every median-run reproduces the frozen Hubble digest,
  and the single-pass chain pair (377.8 vs 375.5) is superseded: its
  0.61% under-read was run-order/thermal noise.

`docs/benchmarks-v1.17.1/d6_phase.csv` holds the probe rows
(instrument: `cargo test -p manifold-codec --release d6_phase_probe
-- --ignored --nocapture`, env `MNF17_P1D6_PHASE_*`; cfg(test),
byte-neutral).
