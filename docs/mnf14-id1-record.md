# MNF14-ID1 — Inverse mathematical description discovery (instrument record)

Work-order thesis: stop proposing geometric families and testing whether
photos happen to fit them; invert instead — start from the RGB pixels and
recover the cheapest decoder-reproducible program, P* = argmin [L(P) +
L(I|P)], evaluated at complete encoded cost (params + residual + framing).
This IS MNF9's original form (ADR-007) generalized to a selectable
vocabulary — and the probe measures exactly the three stages the memo
names. Instrument: `id1_probe.py` (copied verbatim to
`crates/mnf-mans/scripts/mnf14_id1_probe.py`), standing honest protocol
(28-B frame, CD-G relabel, zstd-19, exact recon asserted per record).

## The engine shape (and one forced design correction)

`discover(img)`: propose candidates from a vocabulary → quantize params
to the wire lattice → replay the DECODER-EXACT integer form → honest
complete-record cost → pick = min(MED-complete record, best law arm).

Method-honesty notes (three accounting bugs found and fixed while
building the engine — every number below is post-fix):
1. The plane/cos2 proposers originally fit MED's **error** plane while
   the cost model replayed them as **whole-frame** laws — composition
   semantics mismatched; a plane fixture read 18,859 B instead of ~76.
   ID1's vocabulary is whole-frame; the MED-composed variant is the
   IF3/PG1 line, already measured.
2. A strided pair-consistency test for modular recurrence inversion
   (`v[::37]`) is INVALID — the recurrence relation holds only between
   consecutive states; the honest form is forward verification of the
   whole scan (found when the fixture's own truth failed to invert).
3. Two successive plane **fixtures** clipped (rate ±1/px over a 256-span
   necessarily clips; masked lstsq recovered the unclipped band only).
   Final form: ID1-A fixtures are GENERATED BY THE WIRE LAW'S OWN
   REPLAY of random parameters — membership by construction; discovery's
   task is inverting them. This is HF-A's known-params discipline
   generalized to every fixture stage.

## ID1-A — unknown members of a fixed vocabulary (the engine CAN invert)

| fixture (law-generated) | MED complete record | discovered law (complete record) | delta |
|--------------------------|--------------------:|----------------------------------:|-------|
| plane (law-replay params)| 4,742               | plane law replay **76 B**          | −98.4% |
| raster recurrence (a·v+b mod 251) | 2,240      | exact modular inversion **74 B**   | −96.7% |
| two-component cosine mix | 22,852              | (all arms > MED; const 31,511)     | declined |

The third row IS the fp-LSQ plateau re-measured on a non-member fixture:
the generator mixes two incommensurate frequencies + per-channel amplitudes
(no vocabulary member generated it); the engine correctly declines.

Zero residual on both — the engine recovers the program AND its exact
integer coefficients when a member of the vocabulary generated the data.

**The decisive structural finding**: the FIRST plane proposer (fp-LSQ +
Q12-quantized replay) plateaued at **22,209 B** on the exact same fixture
— rounding-error residuals over 65k pixels compress expensively no matter
how good the fit looks numerically. The exact-generative integer form
(fit a at i16, rates at Q12 landing on the SAME lattice the generator
used) hits zero and wins by 60×. **Law membership is decided by the
generator's rounding convention, not fit quality** — ADR-007's rule,
now proven from the inverse side. Symbolic regression over real-valued
laws CANNOT compete with exact-generative integer rules in a lossless
codec at identical vocabularies; the inverse search's vocabulary must be
exact-generative by construction.

## ID1-B — compositions (region structure inverts per-region)

Two-law composite (top half law-generated plane, bottom half a different
generator) — whole-frame arms LOSE (plane 109,877 / const 64,411 vs MED
35,428): a whole-frame law cannot commit both regions. The PER-REGION
rows prove the engine inverts each region exactly (B-top: plane 72 B vs
MED 3,976 = −98%; the bottom band declines every arm like ID1-C below —
its generator is the non-member cos mix). The wire mechanism for "several laws + region
structure" ALREADY EXISTS: the pick's per-tile record grammar (IF4's
per-tile law arms, modes 6/7). ID1-B's verdict: compositions reduce to
per-region exact laws under the existing grammar — the missing piece is
the per-tile exact-generative vocabulary (div-form at tile scale: a
per-tile (vx,vy,T,D) family is the concrete candidate), NOT a new
whole-frame architecture.

## ID1-C — real photographs (3/4 decline; one isolation-estimate signal)

| photo    | MED complete record | best discovered arm | delta (isolation) |
|----------|--------------------:|--------------------:|------------------:|
| astronaut| 405,842             | const 569,120       | declined          |
| coffee   | 439,143             | const 599,211       | declined          |
| chelsea  | 331,079             | const 539,160       | declined          |
| hubble   | 486,037             | const **439,798**   | **−46,239 (−9.5%)** |

The engine's vocabulary cannot compress astronaut/coffee/chelsea — the
R0/GSE-02 floors hold (photographic structure is not the
arithmetic/periodic/affine class; residual entropy after prediction is
not geometric).

**hubble's −9.5% is an ISOLATION estimate, not a promotion result** (the
R1 lesson: pilot numbers are never quoted against the pick). Mechanism:
hubble = black sky + sparse stars; MED converts the black field into a
±noise-plane (expensive to compress) while zero-prediction keeps literal
zero-planes that zstd RLEs away. The candidate is the trivial "flat
field" law — MNF9's constant family exists (ADR-007) and the shipped
pick priced it against hubble's actual 486,009 and declined it, so the
shipped framing (whole-frame header + residual) differs from this
probe's (28-B frame + CD-G plane). The honest next rung: a Rust
const-arm slice riding the shipped pick's framing (per IF4's admission
contract: five sites, pack-shape, corpus gate vs 812,180, full revert on
tie) — if the corpus gate moves hubble-like content without regression,
it promotes; if it ties, the wire stays untouched per ADR-010.

## Scope of closure

CLOSED:
- fp-LSQ/rounded-replay law vocabularies cannot win losslessly at any
  fixture scale (the rounding floor; four rows measured).
- naive (stride-based, unwrapped-LSQ) recurrence inversion fails on
  modular laws; forward-verified exact modular inversion WORKS and is
  the retained form.
- whole-frame vocabularies on region-composed content (must dispatch
  per region; the per-tile grammar is the dispatch).
- the engine inverts its own vocabulary exactly when the fixture is a
  vocabulary member (ID1-A 2/2) — discovery quality is NOT the
  bottleneck; vocabulary-exactness and firing-class presence are.

OPEN (the honest next rungs, in priority order):
1. **Rust const/flat-field arm under the shipped framing** — hubble's
   class (black-heavy / zero-run content) measured by the corpus gate;
   the cheapest possible admission slice, IF4's recipe applies verbatim.
2. **Per-tile div-form law family** (tile-scale (vx,vy,T,D) exact
   generators inside modes 6/7 competition) — ID1-B's composition
   verdict makes this the composition-capable rung; MNF9's solver
   discipline (bounded D scan + interval intersection) applies at tile
   scale.
3. **Composition-aware discovery** (the memo's ID1-C form): a search
   that PROPOSES region boundaries (cheap: quadtree over residual-cost
   maps — IF3's tree) AND exact per-region laws — the union of (1)
   and IF3's machinery, gated by the same contract.

NOT closed: arbitrary-structure symbolic regression over real-valued
programs (YAGNI until a firing class justifies it — every measured
step of that direction hit the rounding or transport floor first).

The memo's core inversion is CORRECT and now measured: discovery-beats-
proposal happens ONLY when (a) the vocabulary is exact-generative
integer (rounding conventions decide), (b) the fixture's class is in
the vocabulary's firing territory, (c) the cost accounting is honest
whole-plane. The shipped pick is already the engine for (a)+(c) inside
its existing families; the two Rust rungs above are the cheapest honest
tests of whether the corpus gains vocabulary it lacks.
