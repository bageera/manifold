Verdicts (all bit-exact on the rendered stack; the render is DECLARED):
1. At 52% rendered-nonzero, the sparse-event mode LOSES to dense LZ —
   the density regime where the record list pays is BELOW ~5-10% nonzero:
   the dose regime of the EER paper (small wire) is genuinely low-dose;
   ours (7 e/px/s, super-res render) is not.
2. The vendor's EER wire ≈ dense-z9 at THIS dose. Zero codec item.
3. Inter-frame motion at super-res granularity ≈ ≪1 px → the phase
   machinery (which needs ≥1-pixel drift) has no signal here; its ~3%
   is an alignment-noise floor, not compression.
