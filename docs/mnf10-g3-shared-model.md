## Findings

1. **Arm C on the shipping coder LOSES**: the atlas-ref arm (+479 B) and
   the dedicated shared-template grammar (+880 B) both LOSE to the z19
   container's own LZ layer, which codes the six exact repeats for FREE.
   The residual-atlas fired only 4 of 24 tile refs — the residual block
   keyed on tile-relative residuals whose first column depends on the
   tile's left neighbor, so 20 exact-PIXEL copies have different
   residual blocks (the mechanism boundary: content-equality of residuals
   ≠ content-equality of pixels).
2. **The zstd-disabled control flips the verdict**: under zlib-9's small
   window the same shared-template arm WINS ~6.5 KB (26,354 vs 32,868) —
   the win belongs to a WEAK container's dictionary, not to the atlas
   grammar. Structural modeling vs the strong container's repetition
   machinery: the container wins on this fixture class.
3. **Capability established, practical value bounded**: a shared-model
   grammar CAN represent 6 exact translations for ~30 B of metadata
   (site coords + markers) + one 3,072-B template — but the already-
   present LZ layer does it cheaper inside z19. The practical-value
   question for G3 shifts to classes where the container's dictionary
   cannot reach: streams longer than the z19 window, distributed
   references, transformed (non-identity) repeats (Arm D), and
   corrupted-reference residuals (Arm E — the 25,500-B corrupted fixture
   stands ready).

## MNF11 (opened 2026-10-28): Residual Structure Discovery — diagnostic 1

Per Jon's Priority-1 directive (measurement before any new coder): the
top photographic record-stream contributors were analyzed (slate,
dusk, forest, ocean, sand, slate-2, terrain) with four
pre-registered questions.

**Q1 entropy**: photographic residual planes carry H ≈ 4.2-5.5
bits/byte per channel; conditioning on the left neighbor recovers
only ~0.3-0.35 bits (the predictor's context already owns most of it).

**Q2 cross-channel dependence**: MI(R,G) ≈ MI(G,B) ≈ MI(R,B) ≈
1.6-2.5 bits — the residual channels share roughly half their entropy.
This is the largest measured unused structure.

**Q3 short-repeats census**: 4-byte grams repeat 3.5k-10k times in the
plane; 6-byte ~0.2-1.7k; ≥8-byte ≈ 0 — the container's string layer
already sees the repeats that exist; residual-level LZ content is
exhausted beyond 6 bytes.

**Q4 alternative symbol organizations**: measured complete streams
through the SAME containers (z19 + z9): [G, R−G, B−G] symbol
organization beats interleaved RGB planar on every photographic file
(−6%..−20%); YCoCg-on-residual in-between; left-diff-of-residual
LOSES.

  language cannot express its 2-D periodicity at wire cost),
  document/poster/screenshot/blocks/repeat/solid

**Verdict (v1, closed): program discovery in this language loses
everywhere it counts** — the shipped grammar's framing economy plus
its token/template machinery covers every real fixture; composition
adds no reachable class that the six MNF9 families + the record/token
arms miss, at least not at v1's wire cost.

Instrument lessons carried to the next moonshots (X2+):
1. Any candidate arm must compose INSIDE the shipped per-tile/token

2. The decomposition check (does the claimed contribution survive
   its component's container cost measured alone?) is mandatory;
   the noise rec "win" was caught exactly this way.
3. MNF9's framing economy sets a brutal bar for whole-frame
   descriptions: new whole-frame languages must include MNF9's own
   framing rules (single record, shared channels) or they lose by
   construction.

The moonshot lab continues (X2 dynamics next) under those
instrument rules.
